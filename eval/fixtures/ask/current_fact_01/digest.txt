Good morning. In markets, the Federal Reserve held interest rates steady at five and a quarter percent yesterday, but Chair Powell signaled that a rate cut could come as early as the September meeting if inflation data continues to cool. The ten-year Treasury yield dropped to four point one percent in response. Wall Street largely expects two rate cuts before year-end.

In world news, India's general election results are being tallied today, with exit polls suggesting Prime Minister Modi's BJP alliance will secure a strong majority. Official results are expected by this evening. Markets in Mumbai rallied three percent ahead of the count.

In science, the World Health Organization issued a new advisory on H5N1 bird flu after a cluster of human cases was detected in Vietnam. The WHO stressed the risk to the general public remains low but urged countries to increase surveillance at poultry farms.
