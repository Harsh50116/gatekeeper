Good morning. In tech, Anthropic raised a new funding round of three billion dollars led by Google, valuing the company at sixty billion dollars. CEO Dario Amodei said the funds will go toward scaling their Claude model family and expanding safety research. The round comes amid fierce competition with OpenAI and Google DeepMind in the frontier AI race.

In world news, the United Nations General Assembly voted to grant Palestine full member state status, with one hundred and forty-three countries voting in favor. The United States and Israel voted against, with twelve abstentions. The decision is largely symbolic but carries diplomatic weight.

In markets, Bitcoin surged past ninety thousand dollars for the first time, driven by institutional inflows following the approval of spot Bitcoin ETFs in several Asian markets. Ethereum also hit a new high of five thousand two hundred dollars.
