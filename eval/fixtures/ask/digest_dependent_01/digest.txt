Good morning. In markets, Stripe filed its S-1 yesterday, finally setting the stage for what could be the largest tech IPO since Arm's listing last year. The company reported twenty-two billion dollars in revenue for the trailing twelve months, with a net profit margin of fourteen percent. The expected valuation range is between eighty and one hundred billion dollars. Early investors including Sequoia and Andreessen Horowitz stand to make significant returns.

In tech, GitHub announced Copilot Workspace, a new AI-powered development environment that goes beyond code completion to handle entire project planning and implementation. The tool is priced at forty dollars per month for individual developers. Stack Overflow reported a forty percent decline in traffic over the past year, which many attribute to the rise of AI coding assistants.

In sports, the FIFA Club World Cup draw was completed, placing Real Madrid and Flamengo in the same group. The tournament begins June fifteenth in the United States, with thirty-two clubs competing across twelve venues.
